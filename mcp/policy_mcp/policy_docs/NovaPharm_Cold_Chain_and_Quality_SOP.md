# NovaPharm Quality — Cold Chain & Temperature Excursion SOP
SOP ID: QA-CC-014
Version: 5.1
Effective date: 2026-02-15
Status: Synthetic test document for SupplySync

## 1. Temperature excursion
Any shipment with a documented temperature excursion must be placed on QUALITY HOLD immediately. The shipment must not be released based solely on an automated recommendation.

## 2. QA disposition
Quality Assurance must review excursion duration, minimum and maximum temperature, product stability data, packaging qualification, and logger evidence before disposition.

## 3. Release
Only QA may authorize release after a documented disposition. An AI agent may collect evidence and recommend an action but cannot approve the quality disposition.

## 4. Disposal
Disposal of a quarantined pharmaceutical product requires QA authorization and an auditable disposal record.

## 5. Supplier notification
Supply Operations may notify the supplier and request investigation documents while the product remains on hold.
